# Data license status

No benchmark-data license is granted by the current camera-ready pre-release.

The Apache-2.0 license in the repository root applies to repository code, not to unreleased interaction data, participant text, third-party excerpts, annotations, or model outputs.

A data license or controlled-use agreement will be selected only after:

- participant consent is verified for the intended release scope;
- third-party content and model-output redistribution terms are reviewed; and
- the de-identified public export is finalized.

The fully synthetic file under `examples/` may be reused under the repository's Apache-2.0 license.
